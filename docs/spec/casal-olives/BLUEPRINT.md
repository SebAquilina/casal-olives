# Casal Olives — Blueprint (Standard tier)

> **Status:** as-built. Live at `https://casal-olives-web.pages.dev/`.
> Custom domain: `casalolives.concierge.studio` — pending parent zone.

## Project charter

A concept-site portfolio piece for `concierge.studio`. Casal Olives is a
fictional single-estate olive-oil house in Mġarr — three siblings, 12
acres, the 1965 stone press their grandfather installed. The site
demonstrates what €4,800 / Standard tier buys: 5 pages, a 5-SKU
catalogue, a journal with 6 seed posts, a real concierge ("Olive"),
and a Saturday tasting booking flow. Two distinct concierge interaction
shapes — sell oil, book tasting — on one site.

## Hard rule (verbatim per skill SKILL.md)

> **No purchase, transfer, paid sign-up, paid plan upgrade, financial
> trade, or money movement is ever executed by an agent on the user's
> behalf without explicit confirmation in chat from the user, on a
> per-action basis. Pre-authorisations do not carry over. Implicit
> consent does not exist.**

For Casal Olives: checkout disabled. Tasting "bookings" don't take
payment — they email `portfolio@concierge.studio` and the operator
confirms manually. The Cart route exists but routes through
`/checkout` (disabled-state page).

## Concept-site disclosures (mandatory)

1. Concept site by `concierge.studio` (Standard tier).
2. Brand is invented — Casal Olives is not a registered Maltese
   business. No VAT/MBR.
3. Projects are illustrative — the polyphenol numbers are realistic
   for Bidni cultivar oil but not from a real lab test.
4. Checkout is disabled — no oil is sold; tasting requests reach a real
   inbox at `portfolio@concierge.studio`.
