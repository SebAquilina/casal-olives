# 01 — Design system

## Voice

- **Specific over poetic.** "Picked October 7-10 from the south slope" beats "lovingly hand-picked."
- **Short.** Two-sentence paragraphs are the norm.
- **Italianate without faking it.** A few Italian words (frantoio, raccolta) — no faux-Italian salesmanship.
- **No food-marketing clichés.** Banned: "lovingly", "passion", "journey", "artisan", "premium", "experience" (verb), "elevate", "curated".
- **First person plural** for the brand ("we"). **First person singular** for opinions ("I'd put Verde on the salad first").

## Type

- **Display:** Playfair Display (italic available) — old-Mediterranean trade-house feel.
- **Sans:** Inter — body and UI.
- **Mono:** ui-monospace — harvest dates, acidity, polyphenols on bottle labels and lab tables.
